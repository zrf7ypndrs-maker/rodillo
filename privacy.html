Privacy policy

Rodillo is a personal indoor-training app used by its author only. It is not offered to other users.

When connected to WHOOP, Rodillo reads recovery, sleep and cycle data for the author's own account. That data is stored only in the author's private GitHub repository and is used only to show recovery and adjust the day's workout. It is never sold, shared or sent to any third party.

Access can be revoked at any time from the WHOOP app. Contact: ajlagos@uc.cl
